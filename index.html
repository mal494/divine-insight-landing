Philosophy

Ancient wisdom, modern craft

Tarot has guided reflection for centuries. Divine Insight treats it with respect — and with engineering rigor. Card data is versioned like software, spreads are scored with transparent positional and elemental weights, and every experience is designed to feel calm, beautiful, and honest.

  • One truth: a single versioned dataset feeds every branch.
  • Transparent scoring: spread meanings you can inspect, not black boxes.
  • Crafted UX: calm visuals, thoughtful motion, accessible by default.
